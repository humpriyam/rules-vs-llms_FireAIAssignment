INCOME-TAX RULES, SALARIED INDIVIDUALS, FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
Sources: Income-tax Act 1961 as amended by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024. All amounts in rupees.
Assumption: resident individual, salary income only.

[S1] SECTION 115BAC(1A): NEW REGIME (default) SLABS
Total income up to 3,00,000: nil
3,00,001 to 7,00,000: 5%
7,00,001 to 10,00,000: 10%
10,00,001 to 12,00,000: 15%
12,00,001 to 15,00,000: 20%
Above 15,00,000: 30%
Slabs are the same for all ages.

[S2] OLD REGIME SLABS (Finance Act 2024, First Schedule, Part III)
Below 60 years: up to 2,50,000 nil; to 5,00,000 5%; to 10,00,000 20%; above 10,00,000 30%.
Age 60 to 79 (senior citizen): up to 3,00,000 nil; to 5,00,000 5%; to 10,00,000 20%; above 30%.
Age 80 and above (super senior): up to 5,00,000 nil; to 10,00,000 20%; above 10,00,000 30%.

[S3] SECTION 16(ia): STANDARD DEDUCTION
New regime: 75,000 (or gross salary if lower).
Old regime: 50,000 (or gross salary if lower).

[S4] SECTION 10(13A) READ WITH RULE 2A: HRA EXEMPTION (old regime only)
Exemption is the least of:
(a) actual HRA received;
(b) rent paid minus 10% of salary;
(c) 50% of salary if living in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai, else 40%.
"Salary" = basic + dearness allowance forming part of retirement benefits.
Exemption cannot be negative.

[S5] SECTION 80C (old regime only): maximum deduction 1,50,000.

[S6] SECTION 80CCD(1B) (old regime only): additional NPS deduction, maximum 50,000, over and above 80C.

[S7] SECTION 80D (old regime only)
Self, spouse and children: up to 25,000; up to 50,000 if the taxpayer is a senior citizen (60+).
Parents: additional up to 25,000; up to 50,000 if parents are senior citizens.

[S8] SECTION 24(b) (old regime only): interest on loan for self-occupied house, maximum 2,00,000.

[S9] SECTION 87A: REBATE (resident individuals)
New regime: if taxable income <= 7,00,000, rebate = tax, maximum 25,000.
  Marginal relief: if income exceeds 7,00,000, tax payable cannot exceed income minus 7,00,000.
Old regime: if taxable income <= 5,00,000, rebate = tax, maximum 12,500. No marginal relief.

[S10] SURCHARGE (Finance Act 2024, First Schedule), on income tax before cess
Total income above 50,00,000 up to 1,00,00,000: 10%
Above 1,00,00,000 up to 2,00,00,000: 15%
Above 2,00,00,000 up to 5,00,00,000: 25%
Above 5,00,00,000: 37% (old regime). New regime is capped at 25%.
Marginal relief: tax plus surcharge cannot exceed tax at the threshold plus the income above the threshold.

[S11] HEALTH AND EDUCATION CESS: 4% on (income tax + surcharge), both regimes.
