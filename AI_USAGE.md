# AI Usage

Tools: Google Antigravity (code scaffolding), Gemini (rule-text drafting, LLM implementations).

## One place the AI was wrong
Gemini returned FY 2023-24 new-regime slabs (3-6L at 5%, 6-9L at 10%, 9-12L at 15%) and a Rs 50,000
new-regime standard deduction for FY 2024-25. The correct FY 2024-25 values (Finance (No.2) Act 2024)
are slabs 3-7L 5%, 7-10L 10%, 10-15L as per the table in the rule text, and a Rs 75,000 standard deduction.
It also omitted the senior and super-senior old-regime slabs and the 80D senior variant for self.
I caught it by comparing against the Income Tax Department's published slabs before writing any code.
